Databricks has established itself as one of the leading enterprise data and AI infrastructure companies by unifying data engineering, analytics, machine learning, and AI workloads on a single platform. The company's positioning around the Lakehouse architecture has enabled it to become a strategic platform for large enterprises modernizing their data stack.

With generative AI driving increased enterprise investment in data infrastructure, Databricks is well positioned to capture incremental platform spend through AI tooling, governance, and model lifecycle management.

Our initial view is positive. While valuation is expected to remain demanding, the business demonstrates characteristics consistent with category-defining infrastructure companies.

**Recommendation:** Proceed to full commercial and technical diligence.
