Databricks is the market leader in enterprise data and AI infrastructure, pioneering the **Lakehouse** architecture that unifies data lakes and data warehouses into a single platform. The company has expanded beyond analytics into machine learning, governance, data engineering, vector search, and generative AI infrastructure, positioning itself as a foundational platform for enterprise AI adoption.

Unlike point solutions focused on a single layer of the AI stack, Databricks owns the entire enterprise data lifecycle—from ingestion and transformation to model development and production deployment.

Our current view is that Databricks represents one of the strongest infrastructure businesses benefiting from the long-term adoption of enterprise AI.
