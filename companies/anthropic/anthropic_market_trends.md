Artificial intelligence has entered a transition phase from experimentation toward enterprise deployment.

The first wave of generative AI adoption was dominated by consumer applications and productivity assistants. The next phase is expected to focus on embedding AI into mission-critical enterprise workflows, creating demand for reliable foundation models, AI infrastructure, and agent platforms.

Anthropic is positioned within one of the largest technology transitions of the decade: the emergence of AI systems capable of performing complex knowledge work.

The investment thesis depends on whether foundation model providers can evolve from model vendors into durable enterprise platforms.
