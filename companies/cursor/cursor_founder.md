Cursor was founded by a technically strong team with deep experience in developer tools, artificial intelligence, and software infrastructure.

The founding team's primary strength is product intuition: identifying how AI can fundamentally change developer workflows rather than simply adding AI features to existing tools.

The team's early execution demonstrates strong founder-product fit, particularly in building products for technical users and rapidly iterating based on developer feedback.

Key diligence areas include scaling enterprise capabilities, maintaining differentiation as AI coding becomes competitive, and building organizational depth beyond the founding team.

### Strong Product Intuition

The team demonstrated an ability to understand emerging developer workflows before the market matured.

Cursor's success has been driven less by proprietary model research and more by exceptional product decisions:
- Context-aware editing
- Repository understanding
- Developer-first UX
- Fast iteration

### Technical Credibility and Speed

The founding team has strong engineering backgrounds, which creates credibility with Cursor's primary users: software engineers, developers, and technical founders.

Cursor has also demonstrated unusually fast product iteration through frequent releases, rapid incorporation of user feedback, and the ability to adapt as frontier models improve.

This is critical in an AI market where capabilities change quickly.

### Limited Enterprise Experience

The company has demonstrated strong developer adoption but has less evidence of enterprise sales execution, security programs, compliance maturity, and large customer procurement.

Scaling from developers to enterprises will require additional leadership.

### Dependence on External AI Models

Cursor benefits from advances made by Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google. Potential risks include reduced differentiation, model providers entering the market, and pricing pressure.

The company needs durable advantages beyond model access.

Cursor's founders demonstrate strong founder-market fit for AI developer tools.

The primary diligence question is whether the team can evolve from an exceptional developer product company into a durable enterprise software platform.

### Recommendation

Continue engagement.

Founder quality is a significant positive factor in the investment thesis.

Priority should be validating:
1. Enterprise execution capability.
2. Long-term product differentiation.
3. Ability to build defensibility beyond foundation model access.
