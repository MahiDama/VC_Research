Figma has become the leading collaborative design platform, transforming how product teams design, prototype, and collaborate. What began as a browser-based design tool has evolved into a broader product development platform serving designers, product managers, engineers, marketers, and increasingly AI-assisted workflows.

The company benefits from exceptional product adoption, strong network effects, and deep integration into enterprise software development processes. While competitive pressure from Adobe, Canva, and emerging AI-native design tools continues to increase, Figma remains the default collaboration platform for modern product organizations.

**Recommendation:** Proceed to strategic diligence with emphasis on AI roadmap, enterprise expansion, and long-term platform opportunities.

The investment case depends on Figma's ability to maintain category leadership while expanding into adjacent product workflows.
