The product development process is undergoing a major transformation as companies move toward more collaborative, cross-functional workflows.

Historically, design, engineering, and product management operated through disconnected tools and handoff processes. Modern software development requires tighter collaboration between these functions, creating demand for unified platforms.

Figma is positioned at the center of this transition by expanding from a design tool into a broader product development collaboration platform.

The investment thesis depends on whether Figma can evolve from the dominant design collaboration tool into the operating system for digital product creation.
