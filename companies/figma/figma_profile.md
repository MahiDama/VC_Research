## Chunk 1 — Executive Summary

Figma is the leading collaborative design platform transforming how digital products are conceived, designed, reviewed, and delivered.

The company disrupted traditional desktop-based design workflows by introducing a browser-native, multiplayer collaboration model that enabled designers, product managers, engineers, and stakeholders to work together in real time.

Figma has evolved beyond a design tool into a broader product development platform spanning design, collaboration, developer handoff, presentations, and AI-assisted workflows.

The company's long-term opportunity is to become the operating system for digital product creation, connecting creative teams with engineering and business stakeholders.

Our current view is that Figma has built one of the strongest collaboration platforms in enterprise software. Key diligence areas include AI disruption risk, enterprise expansion, and whether Figma can maintain differentiation as interface generation becomes increasingly automated.
